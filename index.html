Our story

Two ways to start.
Both slow.

Aurora Bean is a small atelier built around two drinks we could never find done properly: cold brew steeped for eighteen hours, and matcha whisked to order.

No syrups from a pump, no shortcuts. We pour in front of you, layer by layer, because the way a drink is made is part of how it tastes.
